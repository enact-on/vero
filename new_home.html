Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our data architecture, compliance, and onboarding.

Where does Vero source its buying intent data? expand_more

Vero indexes first-party intent (your website traffic, docs visits, product signups) alongside third-party behavioral networks tracking content consumption across 85+ B2B research portals, public job boards, SEC disclosures, patent filings, and cloud infrastructure telemetry.

How does the waterfall contact enrichment work? expand_more

Instead of relying on a single vendor database that suffers from 30% decay, Vero queries 14 premier data providers in sequential milliseconds. We verify each email via real-time SMTP handshake and validate phone lines before charging a single credit.

Can Vero write directly back to our CRM? expand_more

Yes. Vero offers deep bidirectional synchronization with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics. You can automatically create or enrich accounts, update custom fields, log AI activities, and assign tasks to rep owners with zero deduplication errors.

Is Vero compliant with SOC2 and global privacy regulations? expand_more

Vero is SOC2 Type II certified and fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA regulations. We do not store sensitive consumer PII, provide immediate automated opt-out mechanisms, and encrypt all stored data with AES-256 both in transit and at rest.

How long does initial setup and onboarding take? expand_more

Standard self-service setup takes less than 15 minutes to connect your CRM and email domains. For enterprise organizations requiring custom scoring model calibration and multi-seat permissions, our dedicated onboarding engineers will have your team live in under 7 business days.

Can our SDRs review AI pitches before they are sent? expand_more

Absolutely. Vero provides full control: you can run in "Autopilot" for trusted routine outreach, or "Copilot Review" where every generated brief, email draft, and LinkedIn prompt sits in an SDR approval queue ready for 1-click edits.
